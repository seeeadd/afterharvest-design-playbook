# Claude in this repo

This is Sean's design playbook for every client: graphics, motion, video and editing, and how a job runs.
Read `README.md` first, then `SOP.md` and the files for the job. The newest lines of `feedback-log.md` are the
freshest rules.

When Sean corrects or approves something in any client chat, the rule lands here (SOP section D): a line in
`feedback-log.md`, repeated corrections promoted into the rule files, client-only facts in `clients/<client>.md`.

Standing rules: no em dashes in copy, no model names in commits, don't create PRs unless asked.
